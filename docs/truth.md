## GCAT's satellite catalogue

Read uniformly, for every object and every year, with no regard to the detector (`scripts/truth/parse-gcat-satcat.ts`). A piece is a row typed as a component (`C`) or fragmentation debris (`D`) whose parent is a standard-catalogue object (`S`) or an auxiliary-catalogue one (`A`, keyed by its launch) and whose separation date is stated. Its kind is its SatType's: fragmentation debris is a breakup, anomalous debris (subtype `J`) an anomalous event, an aerodynamic breakup (`Q`) aerodynamic, slag (`K`), coolant (`N`), debris released at orbit insertion (`O`) and every other component a release. A release separated by the end of the day after its launch day is the launch's own staging and makes no statement; a separation dated before its parent's launch is a slip and makes none. The pieces of one parent whose separation windows lie within 3 days of each other (GCAT's own `?` on a day) are one separation and one statement, dated as GCAT dates most of them, its kind the first of breakup, anomalous, aerodynamic and release among them. The statements then join the other sources' events by the build's rule (same parent, windows within thirty days, `SAME_EVENT_DAYS`, never two separations it dates more than a day apart, each joining the instant nearest it); where another source states the event, that source's instant, kind, orbit, cause and count stand, and the satellite catalogue's are kept beside them. An event it alone states is of confidence C where GCAT dates it to the month or finer, D where to a year.
