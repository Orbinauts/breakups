# The events, dissected

Generated by `npm run kpi -- --aggregate` with the scorecard, over the years 2005 to 2026 of the Archive with the confirmed-v10 detector: one row per scoreable reported event, with the Confirmed line's outcome and its cause first (the target since the seventh decision record), then the parent-step line's outcome, the cause as the detector's own log for the parent gives it, the alert's lead over the catalogue's first fragment, whether and when the alert was confirmed, and the timing error where the truth states a minute. Never edited by hand. The validation years are the odd ones; 2005, the warm-up year, is replayed and listed but scored on no split, and the totals leave it out.

The Confirmed line's causes: **confirmed** (the first confirmed verdict by pieces on the parent within 180 days: attributed from the pieces, or the step alert confirmed by them, with the days after the event, after the first fragment's publication and before or after the first agency statement); **confirmed at the launch** (a miss on the body named, confirmed only with the twins matched at the launch: a verdict naming the launch with the parent among its candidates); **no fragments catalogued** and **no parent orbit** (not a target: no fresh piece in the parent's plane within 180 days, or no set of the parent before the event to test a plane against); and for a miss, read off the detector's attribution rows for the launch in the 180 days after the event, the first that applies: **ambiguous parent** (the pieces lay in the plane of another body of the launch too and did not converge on the parent clearly better), **old cloud** (the launch remembered a cloud within the year and the parent or the pieces lay in its plane), **young launch** (under a month old), **backlog** (the launch was still cataloguing an old cloud), **velocity floor**, **single piece**, **fresh piece** (a debris-classed parent itself a fresh piece), **too few pieces**, **no convergence** (propagated back, the pieces missed the parent), **not timed**, **analyst range** (the pieces were analyst objects), **cataloguing batch** (the run listed new bodies under many old launches at once), **no common origin** (the pieces' own closest approaches to the parent, propagated back, did not share an instant), **a crewed station** (a station's launch, whose pieces are released on purpose), **a crewed or cargo craft** (the launch of a crewed spacecraft or a station's cargo craft, the parent not its rocket body: released on purpose too), **reentered** (the parent had reentered by its pieces' first set: reentry debris), **small batch** (three or four pieces not sharing a tight common origin); **attributed to another body** (the launch's pieces were attributed to another of its bodies); **parent not a candidate** (pieces were judged for other bodies of the launch, or lay in some plane, but never for the parent: a debris-classed parent, or one the detector held no set of); **no piece in a plane the detector read**; **no new body seen** (none under the launch in the window, as when the pieces were catalogued as analyst objects).

The causes: **raised** (detected, with what raised it); **no post set before the catalogue** and **persistence after the catalogue** (late: the parent's first set after the event came after the first fragment's, or came before it but the second or third did not); **record gate** (a persistent step the object's own record classed as a manoeuvre); **manoeuvre explanation** (a persistent step on an object with no record of moving itself that an explanation from outside the record read as a manoeuvre: its working status at the step, a return to a held altitude, siblings or a deployment); **ramping fit** (the step held at the post sets' centroid but not at the first post set); **step taken back** (the later sets undid the first jump); **below persistence** (a candidate that faded under the gates); **no further set** (a candidate that lapsed); **margin** (a persistent step on the parent within ten days of the window but outside the matcher's margin); **no sets before the catalogue** (no set of the parent between the event and the first fragment); **parent did not move** (the parent had sets and none left its model); **no public sets** (undetectable, out of the denominator).
