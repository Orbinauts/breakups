## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The detector's first phase, `step-v1`, the parent-step channel on every object in every orbit: `src/series.ts` keeps a ring of each object's last thirty-two sets with the one-step-ahead residual each left, de-duplicates re-published epochs, slots older epochs in, rejects unreadable sets and holds a set too far from the orbit (fifty kilometres of semi-major axis, half a degree of inclination, five degrees of node off its J2 precession) until the next set confirms it, and measures the object's own scatter (a robust short scatter floored by a half-year memory, a regime prior scaled by the drag term and the day's flux and Ap until there are residuals); `src/model.ts` fits a Huber-weighted local polynomial per regime and predicts with the extrapolation's leverage and a drag term that grows with B* and the gap; `src/environment.ts` reads each run's residuals by band (fifty kilometres of perigee height in low orbit) for the day's common-mode drag and live scale, and the space-weather indices for the storm hold and the prior; `src/step.ts` estimates the step over the post sets with a free settling slope, the later sets' ratio to the first jump, the evidence for a step against a drift drag could produce, and a two-sided CUSUM; `src/run.ts` runs the three passes and raises `watch` on a quiet object's first post set, `suspected` when the step persists across two post sets over eighteen hours (six above low orbit) or three, with three under a storm hold or run stress, and `dismissed` when the next sets undo it; `src/params.ts` holds every threshold, each tagged physical, policy or fitted, with its provenance; `src/log.ts` writes one row per candidate with every signal and the step's trajectory, for the offline sweeps `npm run kpi:sweep` runs over a year's rows.
- `docs/detector/d1-fitting.md`: what was swept on the training split, what was chosen and why.

### Changed

- The scorecard writes and reads a year's file gzipped (`kpi/years/<year>.json.gz`): with every persistent step raised before the manoeuvre classing, a year holds tens of thousands of verdicts and log rows, tens of megabytes plain and a few compressed, and the files are committed. The KPI workflow uploads the compressed file.
- The ground truth: every on-orbit breakup and anomalous event an authority reported, 1961 to today, as `data/breakups.json`, built by `npm run truth` from NASA's History of On-Orbit Satellite Fragmentations (16th edition, read from its text), GCAT's Space Debris Clouds table, and hand-entered statements from NASA's Orbital Debris Quarterly News, a U.S. Space Forces-Space notice, ESA's Space Environment Report, named analysts, news reports and the public catalogue; each row keeps every source's statement verbatim beside the values resolved from them and lists where the sources disagree. The report `docs/truth.md` says what was built from what.
- `readBreakups`, `readStatements` and the types of the truth, exported for whoever reads the file.
- The scorecard under `scripts/kpi/`: `npm run kpi:year -- <year>` replays a year of the Archive through the detector in runs of four hours, in the catalogue's publication order, warmed up from the previous year, and matches what it raised against the ground truth by the rule of the fourth decision record (a detection is an alert on the parent's own step before the first fragment's set and before any notice; late, duplicate, exempt and false alarms apart; parents with no public elements out of the denominator; breakups of confidence A and B on the headline line, coarser confidences and anomalous events on their own); `npm run kpi -- --aggregate` folds the years into `kpi/latest.json` and `kpi/latest.md`, the cells per split (the odd years held out, the even, every year, the last three, and before and from 2018). The KPI workflow recomputes the aggregate over the release's Archive and fails on main when it differs from the committed one. The README's "How well it does" table is read from the aggregate by the tests.
- `ElementSet`, the light row the detector reads, with `fromElements`, `apsidesOf` and `regimeOf`; and `runDetector`, the library's one entry point, with the `Verdict`, `Step`, `Notice` and `SpaceWeatherDay` shapes the scorecard scores. The detector itself is not written yet: this one carries the states through and raises nothing, so the scorecard shows zeros and says so.
- The Archive's tooling under `scripts/archive/`: the layout and columns of a year, the strip of a yearly bundle into them, the pull of the current year from the catalogue's history class (throttled, cached, ledgered), the satellite-catalogue and space-weather snapshots, and the upload and download of the years as release assets with a manifest of hashes.
